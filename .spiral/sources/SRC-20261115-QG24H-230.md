# Confirmed resumable-operation simplification

After CYC223 is accepted, merged and pushed, the maintainer asks “next cycle?”.
The agent rereads the governing broker, suite and high-priority library roadmap
and proposes continuing simplification: ResourceLifecycle is already shared,
but still carries collection-specific IDs while both apps coordinate completion.
The proposed goal is a simpler operation/ack boundary proved with Notebook and
Presenter, checking interrupted moves/deletions through reload, lost replies and
reconnection and preserving each app's deletion/local recovery rules. Drag/drop
and the maintainer's alternative slide-management idea remain deferred.
The maintainer confirms “yes”. This authorizes the cycle, not commits or merging.

Effective behavior is inspected on accepted main: begin/retry create durable
physical operation state, then apps independently finish collection update/sync,
local repair/purge and final acknowledge. Notebook keeps local drafts and refuses
dirty-current destructive operations; Presenter confirmed deletion purges local
copies and can operate without collection membership. Do not homogenize those
policies or substitute physical DELETE for Files' tombstone-only workflow.
