# Raw capability messages and remote-loss settlement need a confirmed contract

Disposition: near-term follow-up proposed; not a release blocker or human-
accepted trade-off. Observed at eac0b1e72e84330f8c8238f05b660a04bec9d439.
EVD147 records the reproductions and DES148 proposes a narrow next cycle.

Observed: PortConnection accepts a 70000-character request and reply even when
serializable refuses the same object; Node also round-trips a Map. Closing only
the remote raw port leaves a timeout:null call pending after the bounded Node
and Chrome observations. Explicit local close then rejects it. No current
frame-level send/receive shape/size validation or remote liveness handler is
present in this module. Application-specific checks and opener/popup monitoring
mitigate some paths but do not establish a universal raw-port guarantee.

Possible consequence: an independent or faulty provider can cross the intended
wire profile, or leave a caller waiting without an automatic settlement path.
This does not demonstrate a disclosure exploit, loss of saved data or failure
of every composed close path. A timeout should not evict a user who is merely
thinking. Reproduce the actual surviving-caller/broker/provider lifecycle before
choosing a detection mechanism; preserve cancel/retry, drafts and original
provider choices without replaying calls. Receiver validation cannot prevent
all allocation cost of structured-clone delivery.

Owner: suite capability transport/presenter integration, with caller domain
validation retained. Proposed mitigation: explicit bounded complete-frame
contract and the smallest evidence-backed peer-loss settlement mechanism, with
independent-peer/browser tests and user-visible recovery. No implementation or
new global service is authorized by this risk record.

application failure or standards violation.
