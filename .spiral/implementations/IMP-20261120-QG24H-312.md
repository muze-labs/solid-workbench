# Isolated assistant and Social Bookmarks fixture

`npm run dev:assistant` starts the preview. Open the printed URL, select a
bookmark and choose **Suggest topics**. The preview hosts a Bookmarks
iframe, which opens its assistant dock and child iframe on demand. The two
apps have separate local origins. Both approvals happen inside the app
that owns the requested operation.

The assistant frame shows the exact selected titles and URLs before its
mock model can run. Correlation IDs stay with the assistant, and Bookmarks
validates the returned IDs and tags. Bookmarks owns review, apply and undo.
Applied tags persist in device-local Turtle.

After topic generation the assistant can call `read-bookmark-selection`.
Bookmarks opens its own labeled modal outside the assistant dock. The
dock stays visible behind the modal while the original call waits. Allow
completes that call with the titles and URLs from the original selection.
Deny or Escape rejects it. The decision applies only to that call, and
disconnect or cancellation clears the pending prompt. Returning
suggestions to Bookmarks is a separate action.

One private `PortConnection` carries both capability directions. The
preview checks expected origins, window identities and per-dock nonces.
The assistant frame is sandboxed and the preview serves framing policies
for each route. Closing the dock disposes the connection and its grants.
The keyboard-resizable dock restores focus to its opener. The carrier
page owns no capability or grant.

The mock preview tests ephemeral authorizations. The planned top-level
assistant tab owns credential setup, model selection and the complete
grant view. Each live assistant frame will coordinate with that tab over
a verified channel for effective settings, grant checks and model requests
across browser storage partitions.
