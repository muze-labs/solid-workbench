test/presenter-browser.mjs waits for the Cobalt contenteditable to be visible
and have positive layout dimensions before measuring its click/tap coordinates.
Existing native pointer, focus and text-propagation assertions remain intact.

After human confirmation of the separately evidenced product gap,
apps/presenter/src/components/regions.mjs clears the previous failed view's
inline visibility style when a replacement provider confirms readiness. The
existing SimplyFlow frame-attribute projection renders the change; no manual
DOM content update, new lifecycle stack, renderer or transport is introduced.
Failed updates and bounded-overflow refusal still hide their unusable surfaces.

viewport resize while disconnected, uses existing Discard/Retry controls,
verifies the saved text, then edits through native touch/mouse and keyboard input.
It runs at phone and desktop widths and captures the recovered UI. The regression
fails against the unchanged product and passes after the readiness-state fix.

## Managed transport and failed-startup release

After the maintainer confirms the additional evidenced gap,
ManagedApp.requestEmbedded reuses serializable(input) at the MessagePort boundary,
matching the standalone broker's plain wire-value handling. It no longer sends
reactive proxies in an embedded capability request.

WorkspaceBroker distinguishes attempted startup from actual content dispatch.
The existing prepare operation can freeze an unstarted view at the broker and
safely acknowledge closure. Both new and pending requests check that freeze
before dispatch; a late readiness result cannot deliver content after closure.
After content delivery, normal provider prepare/refusal remains authoritative.
No new wire operation, discard default, HTTP/auth path or persistence format.

Three unit regressions cover real-channel proxy transport, delayed readiness
versus unstarted preparation, and a delivered view retaining its unsaved-work
veto despite failing to produce ready content. The independent browser fixture
can report startup failure before invocation. Presenter coverage verifies leaving
that failed slide, reopening it, restoring access and Retry, then its ordinary
close veto; managed coverage verifies Disconnect -> switch -> return with native
text editing. Native input/visibility corrections earlier in IMP197 remain.

Final download figures supersede the earlier visibility-only measurements:
Workbench 141261 and Presenter 132695 gzip bytes, complete minified graphs with
auth callback. Shared change adds about 90 gzip B per app; Presenter adds 92 B
versus baseline. Readable warnings remain advisory. No new dependency.
