is not treated as an app guarantee across engines or configurations.

separate windows, the maintainer explicitly chooses the former. All attempted
focus workarounds are removed. The selected behavior remains the previously
verified tab restoration and DOM heading focus; no tab-activation guarantee is
claimed, and there is no popup override or delayed focus loop.
