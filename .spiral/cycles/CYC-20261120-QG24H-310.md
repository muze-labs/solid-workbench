# Prove both assistant permission directions in Social Bookmarks

The maintainer confirms the proposed first assistant cycle. The verified
branch is `spiral/CYC-20261120-QG24H-310-assistant-fixture` from `d9f0827`.
[Source](../sources/SRC-20261120-QG24H-311.md)

The suite has a lazy inspector dock and cross-origin capability transport,
but cannot review a bookmark disclosure, authorize one assistant request,
and return topic proposals for caller-owned review. This continues the
assistant-first sequence after the accepted broker Phase 4 follow-up.
The older Documents-first live-provider proposal is separate.

## Scope and evaluation

- Build a small Social Bookmarks fixture for selected titles and URLs. Use
  an independently served assistant origin and an in-process mock model.
  The application owns manual tags, accepting a proposal, and undo.
- Make one interaction complete on phone and by keyboard. Open the panel
  on demand, disclose the exact selected data, require approval in the
  assistant surface, and support cancellation/revocation and focus return.
  Verify that closing or replacing a session cannot apply a late result.
- Reuse the suite's capability transport and sidebar resize where they
  fit. Keep inspector definitions and channels outside the assistant.
- Prove both capability directions as requested in review. Approving topic
  suggestions does not authorize an assistant call to Bookmarks. Await a
  separate decision before answering the read. Allow continues that same
  call with a bounded result. Deny cancels it, and any later read asks again.
- Show both approvals inside their respective app frames in one tab. Keep
  the assistant on its own origin and reuse the same two-way capability port.
  Keep the Assistant dock visible behind the Bookmarks permission dialog.
  Real provider credentials retain the planned top-level setup boundary.
- Keep selection and proposal transient. Keep accepted and manual fixture
  tags locally in RDF through reload. The manual path remains reachable if
  the assistant origin is unavailable. No Pod or provider credentials.
- Compare ordinary app startup requests and the fixture's first usable
  page and assistant task downloads. Run source/build and browser checks.
  Distinguish automated phone/keyboard checks from physical-device and
  assistive-technology review.

Live providers, full Social Bookmarks, persistent grants, generalized tools
and assistant-driven publication are subsequent work. Replan if the
cross-origin request cannot use the existing scoped transport safely.
