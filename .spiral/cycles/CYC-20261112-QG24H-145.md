caller/provider UI, native state boundaries, vocabulary, code size, setup steps
and user decisions. Map offline broker, untrusted origins, stale providers,
denied access, cancellation, multiple-provider and concurrent-request evidence.
Reuse existing observed tests and collect narrowly targeted disposable probes
when a material question remains. Record exact baseline and fixture boundaries.

Produce EVD147's failure/complexity matrix and DES148's proposed keep/change/
defer decisions, with concrete follow-up cycles and reasons for or against
SHACL, richer manifests, discovery/ranking and URI-valued wire message types.
End when the assessment is reviewable, not when additional features are built.
Tests prove bounded behavior, not usability, third-party certification or human
acceptance. Reproduce and record any consequential defect; product corrections
require a confirmed concrete gap/scope before implementation.

full sharing, signup, iframe Escape repair or AI implementation in this cycle.
